# Quick Start - Project Cost Manager

1. Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
2. Complete the setup screen: language, currency, tax label, tax rates and company details.
3. Add projects in `Projects`.
4. Enter planned costs and revenue in `Budget`.
5. Enter real costs and revenue in `Actuals`.
6. Review margins in `Dashboard` and `Variance`.
7. Create client-facing quotes in `Quotes`.
8. Use `Export Excel` to share results with advisors.
9. Use `Backup JSON` to save a complete restorable copy.

Your data stays on your device. Make JSON backups regularly.
