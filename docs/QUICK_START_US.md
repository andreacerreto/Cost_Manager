# Quick Start - US Version

Project Cost Manager US is a lightweight offline project control tool. It is not a tax application.

## Setup

1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.
2. Use the US-oriented defaults: `USD` and `en-US` number formatting.
3. Add your company details for quote headers and Excel exports.
4. Add projects in `Projects`.
5. Enter planned costs and revenue in `Budget`.
6. Enter real costs and revenue in `Actuals`.
7. Review margins, overheads and variance in `Dashboard`, `Variance` and `Analytics`.
8. Create client quotes in `Quotes`.
9. Use `Export Excel` to share operational data with an accountant, consultant or advisor.
10. Use `Backup JSON` regularly for a complete local backup.

## No Tax Core Policy

Amounts in the app should be treated as pre-tax / tax-excluded values.

The app must not:

- calculate Sales Tax;
- look up tax rates;
- determine taxability;
- manage exemptions, resale certificates or tax-exempt certificates;
- determine nexus, registrations, filing obligations or remittances;
- create a Sales Tax payable/receivable ledger.

Taxes, if applicable, should be handled outside the app by the user, their accountant, their tax advisor or a dedicated tax/compliance tool.

## Recommended Quote Notice

Printed quotes should include this simple notice:

> Amounts shown are pre-tax. Taxes, if applicable, are excluded unless expressly stated.

## Operational Focus

Use the app for:

- project budget vs actuals;
- gross margins;
- overhead tracking;
- variance analysis;
- quote preparation;
- Excel sharing;
- JSON backup and restore.

Do not use the app as a source of tax advice or tax compliance records.
