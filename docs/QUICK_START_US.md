# Quick Start - US Mode

1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.
2. In `Settings`, set `Country / tax profile` to `United States`.
3. Keep the default `USD`, `en-US`, `Sales Tax` and `EIN / Tax ID` settings unless your advisor tells you otherwise.
4. Add your company details and EIN / Tax ID.
5. Add projects in `Projects`, then enter planned costs and revenue in `Budget`.
6. Create client quotes in `Quotes`.
7. For each quote, set the applicable manual Sales Tax rate, customer state/county, and tax-exempt status if relevant.
8. Use `Export Excel` to share the workbook with your accountant or tax advisor.
9. Use `Backup JSON` regularly for a complete local backup.

US Mode is a manual documentation workflow. It does not automatically determine taxability, rates, exemptions, registration requirements, filing requirements, or economic nexus.
